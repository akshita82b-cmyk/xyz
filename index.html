💬 Real Traveler FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions (Real People Queries)

Answers to real questions asked by travelers on Quora, Reddit, and voice search assistants.

❓ How much does Tempo Traveller or Bus hire cost per KM from Zirakpur?

Outstation Tempo Traveller rates start at ₹26/KM for 9 to 12 Seater AC models, ₹28/KM for 17 Seater Maharaja VIP, ₹36 - ₹38/KM for 24 & 26 Seater, ₹40/KM for Force Urbania VIP, and ₹45/KM to ₹65/KM for 27 to 60 Seater Deluxe AC Buses. Minimum billing is 250 KM/day.

❓ Are state border taxes, toll plaza taxes, and parking charges included in the fare?

No, toll taxes, state border entry permits (e.g. Himachal, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan, UP), and parking charges are paid directly at toll booths by the customer or added as actual receipts. Fuel and driver charges are included in our base per-KM quote.

❓ Does the Air Conditioning (AC) stay switched on while driving on steep mountain roads in Himachal or Uttarakhand?

Yes! All our luxury Tempo Travellers and Deluxe AC Buses are equipped with dual heavy-duty roof AC compressors and high-horsepower engines engineered to run climate control smoothly even during steep incline climbing to Shimla, Manali, and Chardham.

❓ What are the driver night allowance (bhatta) and driver food/stay rules for outstation trips?

Driver night allowance is fixed at ₹500 to ₹600 per night for outstation stays. You do not need to arrange separate hotel rooms for the driver; drivers handle their own food and sleep inside the vehicle sleeper compartment.

❓ Can I book a 27 Seater or 50 Seater Deluxe Bus for a wedding baraat from Zirakpur to Delhi or Punjab?

Yes! Bharat Bus Service In Zirakpur specializes in wedding baraat buses, corporate retreats, and pilgrimage tours. Vehicles come with pushback reclining seats, air suspension, ambient music system, and large luggage boots.

❓ What is the booking advance amount and cancellation policy?

You can lock your booking with a nominal 10% to 20% advance token amount via UPI, GPay, PhonePe, or Bank Transfer. The remaining balance is paid to the driver during the trip.

❓ What happens if a vehicle faces a mechanical breakdown during a long outstation journey?

Bharat Bus Service maintains 24/7 roadside assistance support across North India. In the rare event of a breakdown, a replacement vehicle of equal or upgraded seating capacity is dispatched immediately at zero extra charge.

❓ Can I get direct pickup and drop from Chandigarh Airport (IXC) or Chandigarh Railway Station (CDG)?

Yes! We provide 24/7 direct pick-and-drop services from Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport Chandigarh, Chandigarh Railway Station, Mohali, Panchkula, and Zirakpur doorstep locations.
